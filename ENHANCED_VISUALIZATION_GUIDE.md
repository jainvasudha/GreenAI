## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **1. Launch the Enhanced App**
```bash
# Run the enhanced visualization app
streamlit run enhanced_visualization_app.py
```

### **2. Navigate the Interface**
1. **Control Panel** - Configure tracking parameters
2. **Real-time Metrics** - Live updates during tracking
3. **Interactive Tabs** - Click to explore different aspects
4. **Detailed Charts** - Interactive visualizations

### **3. Explore Each Tab**

#### **🌍 Carbon Emissions Tab**
- **Summary Metrics**: Total CO₂, average intensity, renewable percentage
- **Time Series Chart**: CO₂ emissions over time
- **Regional Comparison**: Emissions by geographic region
- **Detailed Table**: Complete CO₂ data with context

#### **⚡ Energy Consumption Tab**
- **Summary Metrics**: Total energy, average consumption, energy rate
- **Time Series Chart**: Energy consumption over time
- **Hardware Analysis**: Energy efficiency by hardware type
- **Utilization Charts**: CPU, GPU, and memory usage patterns

#### **💧 Water Usage Tab**
- **Summary Metrics**: Total water, average usage, environmental equivalents
- **Time Series Chart**: Water usage over time
- **Regional Comparison**: Water usage by geographic region
- **Intensity Analysis**: Water usage vs energy consumption

#### **🌍 Combined Analysis Tab**
- **Multi-metric Overview**: CO₂, energy, and water together
- **Combined Time Series**: All metrics in one chart
- **Regional Impact**: Scatter plot of regional environmental impact
- **Environmental Context**: Trees, car miles, bottles, showers

#### **📚 Calculations Tab**
- **Energy Calculation**: How energy consumption is calculated
- **Water Calculation**: How water usage is determined
- **Carbon Calculation**: How CO₂ emissions are computed
- **Environmental Context**: What the numbers mean
