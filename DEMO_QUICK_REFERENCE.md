## ❓ **Top 5 Questions & Answers**

### 1. **"How accurate is the tracking?"**
> "We use CodeCarbon, the industry standard. It provides reliable relative measurements for comparing different approaches."

### 2. **"What's the performance impact?"**
> "Minimal - less than 2% CPU overhead and 50MB memory. It runs in the background without interfering."

### 3. **"Why this color palette?"**
> "Forest green represents growth, sage represents balance, earth brown represents grounding. It's both professional and environmentally conscious."

### 4. **"How do you calculate environmental equivalents?"**
> "We use established conversion factors: 1 kg CO₂ ≈ 0.06 trees, 1 kg CO₂ ≈ 2.2 car miles. These help users understand the scale."

### 5. **"What's the business case?"**
> "Sustainable AI reduces costs, improves reputation, ensures compliance, and leads to more efficient resource usage."
