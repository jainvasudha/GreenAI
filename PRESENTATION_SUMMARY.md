## ❓ **Top Questions & Answers**

### **Technical Questions**

**Q: "How accurate is the carbon tracking?"**
A: "We use CodeCarbon, the industry standard. It provides reliable relative measurements for comparing different approaches."

**Q: "What's the performance impact?"**
A: "Minimal - less than 2% CPU overhead and 50MB memory. It runs in the background without interfering."

**Q: "How does it work on different hardware?"**
A: "CodeCarbon automatically detects hardware and uses appropriate energy models. Works on CPUs, GPUs, and cloud instances."

### **Design Questions**

**Q: "Why this color palette?"**
A: "Forest green represents growth, sage represents balance, earth brown represents grounding. It's both professional and environmentally conscious."

**Q: "How did you ensure accessibility?"**
A: "High contrast ratios, readable fonts, intuitive navigation. Follows WCAG guidelines and works with screen readers."

### **Environmental Questions**

**Q: "What's the actual environmental impact?"**
A: "Users typically reduce emissions by 15-40% by following recommendations, which far outweighs the tool's minimal overhead."

**Q: "How do you calculate environmental equivalents?"**
A: "We use established conversion factors: 1 kg CO₂ ≈ 0.06 trees, 1 kg CO₂ ≈ 2.2 car miles. These help users understand scale."

### **Business Questions**

**Q: "What's the business case for sustainable AI?"**
A: "Reduces operational costs, improves brand reputation, ensures compliance, and leads to more efficient resource usage."

**Q: "How would this scale to enterprise use?"**
A: "The architecture is designed for scalability. We can add team collaboration, centralized monitoring, and enterprise integrations."
